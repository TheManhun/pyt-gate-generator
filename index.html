    2.

    3.

    4.

    Then, in your mod
    
        
      
          
    1. Save the .con.lua and .script.lua into content/my_gates/, next to your model.
      2. 
          
    2. Add the lines from the _content.json tab to the "files" list in your mod's _content.json. TF3 only loads listed files, so your model's meshes, materials and textures must be listed too.
      3. 
          
    3. In your .mdl, set the collider to type = "NONE" so the gate can stand over a road. TF3's Model Editor resets it to a box on every save.
      4. 
          
    4. Give the entry pictures: gate@2x.tga (menu icon, 240 x 150) and gate_preview@2x.tga (720 x 405) in the same folder. Without a preview the info box shows plain blue.
      5. 
          
    5. Keep every file and folder name lower case (TF3's upload validator warns on capitals), and never rename the model after release: placed gates are saved by model.
      6. 
          
    6. Say "Pay Your Tolls compatible" on your mod page with a link to Pay Your Tolls on mod.io or this page. That credit is all the licence asks for.

    TF3 contract version 1. Pay Your Tolls (TF3) reads only the placed model's file name (it must contain pyt_gate_) and its position. Prices, revenue, saves, naming and the toll window stay with Pay Your Tolls. Each vehicle pays once per 20 seconds, at whichever toll it reaches first, so a gate beside another earns nothing extra. Construction choices arrive 1-based in TF3; the generated script handles it.
