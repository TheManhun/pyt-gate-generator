PYT Gate Generator

Turn your toll gate model into a Pay Your Tolls compatible construction

Fill in the form and the finished .con file writes itself on the right. Nothing is sent anywhere; this page runs entirely in your browser. Save the file into your mod under res/construction/asset/ with the name shown, ship your model as usual, and Pay Your Tolls will find the gate, count every vehicle through it and charge the toll. No script, no dependency: without Pay Your Tolls your gate is simply a decoration.
