PYT Compatibility Licence, version 1
Pay Your Tolls is a Transport Fever 2 mod by Epod.

WHAT THIS COVERS
The Pay Your Tolls compatibility system: the pyt_gate_ file-name
contract, the ways / lanes / pyt_era parameter convention, the PYT Gate
Generator (this page and repository), every construction file the
generator produces, and the example construction in the compatibility
guide.

It does NOT cover the Pay Your Tolls mod itself (its scripts, models,
textures, icons and artwork). Those remain Epod's, and are available
only through the Steam Workshop under its terms.

YOU MAY
- Use the compatibility system in any Transport Fever 2 mod, free of
  charge, including mods you distribute in any way.
- Modify the generated files and the example construction as much as
  you like.

YOU MUST
- Credit "Pay Your Tolls" in your mod's Workshop or mod.io description,
  with a link to the Pay Your Tolls Workshop page
  (https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3800813265)
  or the compatibility page (https://themanhun.github.io/pyt-gate-generator/).
  The suggested wording is simply:

      Pay Your Tolls compatible
      Uses the Pay Your Tolls compatibility system:
      https://themanhun.github.io/pyt-gate-generator/

- Keep the "Pay Your Tolls compatible" header comment in any generated
  or derived construction file.

YOU MUST NOT
- Present the compatibility system, the generator, or a generated
  template as your own original system.

YOU DO NOT HAVE TO
- Make Pay Your Tolls a dependency of your mod, or say it requires
  Pay Your Tolls. It does not; the whole design is that your gate
  works without it.
